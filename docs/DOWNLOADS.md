## Verify a download

The official 1.0.2 distributions are preserved unchanged. Use the [SHA-256 checksums](SHA256SUMS.txt) to verify a download:

```powershell
Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 '.\downloaded-file.zip'
```
