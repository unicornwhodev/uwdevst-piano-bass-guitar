- 9 models in 3 families
- 18 factory presets and an XML preset collection
- Brightness, envelope, filter and drive controls
- Sound generated by synthesis, with no sample library to install

## Choose your bass

| Family | Models | Ideas to play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acoustic-inspired | Double Bass, Fingered Bass, Slap Bass | Played lines, detached notes and string attacks. |
| 808 | Sub 808, Boom 808, Distorted 808 | Deep low end, sustained notes and electronic accents. |
| Synth | Moog Bass, Reese Bass, Acid Bass | Synthesizer bass colours, filter movement and repeating patterns. |



## Play it

Start with a Reference preset and a short line in the bass register. Adjust attack and release for the rhythm, then body and filtering for its place in the mix.

Use the Standalone application with a MIDI keyboard, or add the VST3 to an instrument track in your music software. [Install Bass](INSTALLATION.md).
