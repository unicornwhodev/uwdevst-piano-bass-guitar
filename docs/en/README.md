## Compose with the collection

Choose a preset, play a phrase and shape the sound for its place in your track. Use the three independent instruments together: keys for harmony, bass for the foundation and guitar for rhythm or texture.

For tone, space and movement, explore the eleven effects in [UWdeVST · Musique FX](https://github.com/unicornwhodev/uwdevst-fx).
